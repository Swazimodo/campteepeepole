## How To Donate

The fees that we charge for our camp are set so that they only cover the actual camp activities. They do not cover building programs or camp maintenance. For the upkeep and development of the physical camp itself, we rely on donations.

We are a not-for-profit Registered Charity under the Government of Canada, Canada Revenue Agency. Our registration number is 89548 6322 RR 0001 and you can view our filings on the CRA Website. Receipts for all charitable donations are sent out at the end of the year.

## Donation Funds

By default donations are added to our general fund. This fund is used for the camp needs as decided by the camp board. However, you can also specify how your donation will be used by designating it to the Nelson Rothfus Memorial Scholarship Fund, to support our Executive Director, Ben Werdal and his salary as he relies on a large portion being through support, or to the Building Fund. All of these funds are very important to camp and helping us to serve to the best of our ability!

## Donate on Website via Canada Helps (One Time or Monthly)

You can donate right here on the website by using the form seen to the left hand side of this page. You are able to choose whether to send a one-time donation or send a regular monthly donation, and which fund you would like to donate to. This is the easiest and most convenient way for the camp to receive donations and is done through an organization called 'Canada Helps'. Their mission is to help Canadian Charities raise funds, and all your donations are fully tax receiptable, and you will receive those receipts right away, directly from Canada Helps, rather than waiting until year end as you have to do with other donations. There is a very small fee they take from the donation, but it is very small. This fee is not charged to you as an extra expense, it just comes from what you donate (i.e. If you donate $100, the camp will receive $96.50, Canada Helps receives $3.50). You do have the option, right before you enter payment info, to cover the fee and increase the donation (i.e. send $103.50, instead of $100), but it is up to you. Regardless of how much you send, the camp is very grateful for your donation, and you will receive a tax receipt for the full amount as Canada Helps is also a registered charity. This method is worth it for us as a camp, even though it comes with a very small fee, as it saves us a lot of work and gives us the opportunity to allow you to send monthly donations if you so choose.

## Donate by e-Transfer

You can also make a donation using an e-Transfer through your bank. When doing this, send the transfer to deposit@campteepeepole.org. This address is configured with our bank to automatically accept transfers, so you shouldn’t have to setup any additional security questions. Make sure that in the message section of the transfer you include the word “donation” and optionally the fund. In addition include your address information if you wish to receive a charitable receipt.

## Donate by Cheque

Of course you can still donate the “old fashioned way” by sending a cheque. Make the cheque out to “Camp Teepee Pole” and send it to our Mailing Address Be sure to include the word “donation” and optionally the fund in the memo field or on an attached piece of paper.
